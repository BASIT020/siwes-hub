Everything the digital logbook should do

Purpose-built modules for each part of the SIWES programme, all sharing one secure database - never paper collecting dust.

Daily logs

Students record their activities day by day. Supervisors approve or reject with feedback.

Weekly reports

Weekly summaries with challenges and lessons learned, reviewed week by week.

Attendance

Verified daily presence records with automatic attendance-rate statistics.

Projects

Students register their training project and submit it for supervisor approval.

Announcements

The SIWES office publishes notices to everyone, students or supervisors only.

How it works

From your first sign-in to your approved final report.

1

Activation

Create your account (or have the SIWES office register you) and get placed in a receiving organization with a supervisor.

2

Record

Students log daily activities, hours and skills while supervisors record attendance.

3

Review

Supervisors approve or return logs, weekly reports and the final project with feedback.

4

Complete

Attendance rates, approved logs and project status build a defensible SIWES record.

About SIWES Hub

A Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) management & monitoring portal for host universities and their partner organizations, built as a Computer Science project: Python Django, a REST API and a vanilla web frontend.
